[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// WHY PERFECTGUARD?[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=3]Protect your server with [B]7 anti-cheat detections[/B] tuned to avoid false positives, a full [B]moderation toolkit[/B] and a brand new [B]in-game staff panel[/B]. Ban, mute, freeze, jail, warn, vanish, inspect - everything you need to keep your community safe, in one plugin.[/SIZE]

[HR][/HR]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// NEW STAFF PANEL (2.4.1)[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][B]Home[/B] - players online, reports, violations and active sanctions at a glance, latest violations and sanctions, your staff alerts and vanish switches. Updates live.
[*][B]Players[/B] - search, then a player file with status, violation level per check, warnings, reports, notes and history. Every action from one place.
[*][B]Reports[/B] - player reports and bug reports with their details.
[*][B]Anti-Cheat[/B] - recent violations by check, every threshold and switch with one Save button.
[*][B]Chat[/B] - word blacklist and muted players with Unmute.
[*][B]Announce[/B] - chat, title and notification announcements with a preview to yourself first.
[*][B]Settings[/B] - auto-freeze / auto-jail levels, jail position, language, reload.
[/LIST]
[SIZE=3]Every sanction asks for confirmation and says who, what and for how long. Each button checks the same permission as its command.[/SIZE]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// FEATURES[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]7 Anti-Cheat Detections[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]- [B]Speed[/B] - abnormal movement speed (the limit follows the player's real speed: effects and gear bonuses never trigger it)
- [B]Fly[/B] - unauthorized flight (falling, climbing, swimming, gliders, mounts and creative are ignored)
- [B]FastBreak[/B] / [B]FastPlace[/B] - impossibly fast block breaking / placing
- [B]Reach[/B] - melee hits beyond normal range (bows, projectiles and effects are never counted)
- [B]Nuker[/B] - mass block destruction
- [B]AutoClick[/B] - inhuman, too regular click patterns
All thresholds configurable. Violation levels with optional auto-freeze and auto-jail. Players with [B]perfectguard.bypass[/B] are ignored.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Ban System[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Permanent bans, temporary bans (1 hour to 30 days), IP bans. All with reasons and history.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Mute System[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Permanent and temporary mutes. Word blacklist with auto-blocking.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Jail[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Jailed players are teleported to your jail, kept there, and [B]lose every permission[/B] until released - their own permissions come back exactly as before on release, instantly.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Staff Tools[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]- [B]Vanish[/B] - invisible staff mode
- [B]Freeze[/B] - stop player movement
- [B]Inventory & armor inspect[/B]
- [B]Teleport[/B] - go to a player or bring a player to you
- [B]Warnings[/B] - issue, view and clear
- [B]Player notes[/B] - private staff notes per player
- [B]Staff alerts[/B] - real-time violation alerts[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Reports[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Players report other players and bugs; staff handle them from the panel.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Announcements[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Title, notification and chat announcements from the panel.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Safe data[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Bans, mutes, reports, notes and logs are saved crash-safe (a crash during a save can never empty them).[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// INSTALLATION[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST=1]
[*]Put [B]PerfectGuard-2.4.1.jar[/B] in your server's [B]mods[/B] folder.
[*]Start or restart the server.
[*]Set your jail with the panel (Settings > Set jail here) or [B]/pg setjail[/B], then open [B]/pg menu[/B].
[/LIST]
[SIZE=3]Updating? Just replace the jar: configs, bans, mutes, jails, reports and logs are kept.[/SIZE]
