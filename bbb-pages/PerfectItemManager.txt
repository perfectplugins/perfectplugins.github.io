[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// WHY PERFECTITEMMANAGER?[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=3]Stop editing JSON files to balance your server. [B]PerfectItemManager[/B] gives you one admin interface to change [B]any item's stats, rarity, random tiers, crafting recipes, benches and drops[/B], live, without restarting.

[B]NEW in 2.0:[/B] a completely redesigned interface. A Home screen with one search for everything, five clear sections, one Save button, and a confirmation before anything is deleted.[/SIZE]

[HR][/HR]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// THE INTERFACE[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]HOME[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]One search for items, recipes, NPCs and blocks. A card per section with live counters, and your recent changes (kept after a restart).[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]FIVE TABS, ALWAYS VISIBLE[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]HOME, CRAFTS, ITEMS, DROPS, SETTINGS. "Back" always returns exactly where you were: search, filter, page and selected item.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]ONE SAVE MODEL[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Every change is a draft with a counter, [B]Cancel[/B] and [B]Save[/B]. Close with Esc and your draft is restored next time. Leaving an item with unsaved changes asks Save / Discard / Stay.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]SAFE BY DESIGN[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Every dangerous action (reset an item, clear tiers, restore a vanilla recipe, remove drops, multiply all items, reset all) asks first and tells you exactly what will be deleted.[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[HR][/HR]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// FEATURES[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]EVERY ITEM OF THE GAME[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]ITEMS lists every item, not only craftable ones. Each item has one card with 5 tabs: Rarity & stats, Roll tiers, Recipe, Drops, Restrictions.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]RARITY & STATS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Durability, Damage, Armor, Tool speed and Damage variance, shown as vanilla / current / new value / change. Presets [B]+10 %[/B], [B]+25 %[/B], [B]+50 %[/B], [B]x2[/B], [B]Vanilla[/B], and a live preview of the player tooltip.
Per-attack damage: give each attack of a weapon its own value (stab, swing, signature...).
Item rarity from Common to Legendary, applied to every copy of the item.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#1abc9c][B]ROLL TIERS (RANDOM LOOT)[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Weapons and armor roll their own tier on each copy: [COLOR=#bdc3c7]Common[/COLOR], [COLOR=#2ecc71]Uncommon[/COLOR], [COLOR=#3498db]Rare[/COLOR], [COLOR=#9b59b6]Epic[/COLOR], [COLOR=#f39c12]Legendary[/COLOR]. Each tier has its chance, damage multiplier and number of affixes; Weapon and Armor presets; the chance total is checked (100 %). "Give 1 rolled" to test instantly.
Each rolled copy has its own tooltip with a colored border and its affixes, and keeps its roll through drops, chests, restarts and world changes.
[B]Weapon affixes:[/B] Damage Boost, Critical Chance, Critical Damage, Lifesteal, Predator (bonus against targets under 30 % health), Speed Boost (movement speed while held), Knockback Bonus.
[B]Armor affixes:[/B] Damage Reduction, Health Bonus, Thorns, Knockback Resistance, Regeneration.  [B]Both:[/B] Durability Bonus.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]CRAFTS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Benches with counters, every recipe with an "Active" box, Disable all / Enable all. The recipe card edits tier, craft time, required permission and ingredients.
[B]New in 2.0:[/B] create a recipe for an item that has none, move a recipe to another bench, change the quantity made, replace an ingredient.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]DROPS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Custom drops for any NPC or block: item, min / max quantity, chance. Add them on top of vanilla drops, or replace vanilla drops entirely. From an item card, see and edit which NPCs and blocks drop it.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]RESTRICTIONS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Block dropping or placing an item, with an optional required permission. Players with [B]perfectitemmanager.bypass[/B] ignore restrictions. Perfect for quest items, admin tools and event rewards.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]GLOBAL ACTIONS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]No durability loss for every item, multiply all stats (x2, x5, x10), reset everything. All confirmed first.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]LIVE TOOLTIPS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Weapon stat lines (damage, speed, roll tier) in item tooltips. When you save a change, every connected player sees the new tooltip instantly: no relog, no restart.[/SIZE]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]6 LANGUAGES[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]English, Français, Español, Italiano, Deutsch, Português. The whole interface and the tooltips are translated; change it in SETTINGS.[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// COMMANDS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][B]/itemmanager[/B] - open the Home screen
[*][B]/itemmanager <itemId>[/B] - open the card of an item
[*][B]/itemmanager crafts [bench][/B] - open CRAFTS (optionally on a bench)
[*][B]/itemmanager drops [npc or block][/B] - open DROPS (optionally on a source)
[*][B]/itemmanager tier <itemId> enable|disable|info|give|menu[/B] - manage the roll tiers of an item
[*][B]/itemmanager perattack <itemId>[/B] - open the per-attack damage section
[*][B]/itemmanager reload[/B] - reload the configuration from disk
[*][B]/itemmanager setlang <EN|FR|ES|IT|DE|PT>[/B] - change the language
[*][B]/itemmanager help[/B] - show help
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// PERMISSIONS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][B]perfectitemmanager.admin[/B] - everything
[*][B]perfectitemmanager.menu.crafts[/B] - CRAFTS
[*][B]perfectitemmanager.menu.stats[/B] or [B].menu.quality[/B] - ITEMS
[*][B]perfectitemmanager.menu.drops[/B] - DROPS
[*][B]perfectitemmanager.menu.global[/B] - SETTINGS and global actions
[*][B]perfectitemmanager.bypass[/B] - ignore item restrictions
[/LIST]
[SIZE=3]Sections a player cannot use are locked in the interface.[/SIZE]

[LIST=1]
[*]Put [B]PerfectItemManager-2.0.1.jar[/B] in your server's [B]mods[/B] folder (singleplayer: enable it in your world's mod list).
[*]Start or restart the server.
[*]Type [B]/itemmanager[/B] in game.
[/LIST]
