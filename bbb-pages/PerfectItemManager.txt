[CENTER]
[SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// WHY PERFECTITEMMANAGER?[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[/CENTER]

[SIZE=3]Take [B]full control[/B] over every item in your server. Override stats, crafting recipes, quality levels, block / entity drops, and now [B]roll per-instance tiers[/B] with random affixes on every weapon and armor piece — Common to Legendary, with colored borders, styled tooltips, and dynamic damage multipliers. All from a [B]beautiful in-game UI[/B], in 6 languages, no file editing.[/SIZE]

[HR][/HR]

[CENTER]
[SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// FEATURES[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#1abc9c][B]Tier System per-instance[/B] [I](new in v1.9)[/I][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Every weapon and armor can roll its own tier at drop / pickup / craft (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary). Each rolled instance carries:
— A [B]colored slot border[/B] from the tier
— A [B]damage multiplier[/B] from the tier
— A pool of [B]random affixes[/B] with min/max ranges shown in the tooltip
— A [B]styled per-stack tooltip[/B] with section headers, colored bullets, and roll-range hints[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#1abc9c][B]Weapon affixes[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Damage Boost, Critical Chance, Critical Damage, Lifesteal, Predator (low-HP bonus), Speed Boost, Knockback Bonus.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#1abc9c][B]Armor affixes[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Damage Reduction (cap 80%), Health Bonus (bumps max HP), Thorns (counter damage to attacker), Knockback Resistance, Regeneration (1Hz health regen tick).[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#1abc9c][B]Common affix[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Durability Bonus — bumps the max durability of the rolled item.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Damage Variance[/B] [I](new in v1.9)[/I][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Slider 0-100% — every hit deals ±X% random damage spread, using Hytale's native [I]randomPercentageModifier[/I]. Makes combat feel less robotic.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Per-Attack Damage editor[/B] [I](new in v1.9)[/I][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Tune individual attacks of any weapon independently — set distinct damage for Stab, Thrust, Swing Down, Swing Down Left/Right, Signature attacks, etc. Available via [B]/itemmanager perattack <itemId>[/B] or the EDIT… button in the Stats tab.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Item Stats Override[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Modify damage, durability, armor, attack speed, knockback, armor toughness for [B]any item[/B] in the game. Changes apply instantly.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Crafting Override[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Change or completely remove crafting recipes. Control which benches can craft which items.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Quality Override[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Set custom quality levels per item. Fine-tune your server's item economy.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Drop Rules[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]— [B]Block Drops[/B] — customize what drops when any block is broken
— [B]Entity Drops[/B] — customize what any mob drops on death
Full UI for creating and managing drop rules.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Restrictions[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Block use, drop, equip or place of any item, optionally per-permission. Useful for class-locked gear or staff-only items.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Weapon Tooltips[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Display [B]damage values[/B] directly in item tooltips, plus the new per-instance tier info. Toggleable in settings.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#55FF55][B]Bulk Edit & Global Overrides[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Apply changes to [B]multiple items at once[/B], or set global multipliers to scale stats server-wide.[/SIZE]

[*][SIZE=4][COLOR=#FFAA33][B]Item Creator[/B] [I](coming in v1.9.3)[/I][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Clone an existing item, set a new ID, name, description, stats, quality, optional custom icon / texture / model — save and ship in your asset pack.[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[CENTER]
[SIZE=5][COLOR=#FF6B00][B]// COMMANDS[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[/CENTER]

[TABLE]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager[/TD]
[TD]Open the management UI (Landing)[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager drops[/TD]
[TD]Open the drop rules UI[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager tier <itemId> enable[/TD]
[TD]Enable tier rolls on an item (auto-detects weapon/armor for defaults)[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager tier <itemId> give[/TD]
[TD]Give yourself one freshly rolled instance of the item[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager tier <itemId> menu[/TD]
[TD]Open the Tier Config editor[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager perattack <itemId>[/TD]
[TD]Open the per-attack damage editor[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager reload[/TD]
[TD]Reload all overrides from disk[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD]/itemmanager setlang <code>[/TD]
[TD]Set language (EN, FR, ES, IT, DE, PT)[/TD]
[/TR]
[/TABLE]
