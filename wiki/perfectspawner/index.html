PerfectSpawner

Mob spawners for Hytale servers: a cage for each of 237 creatures, a page on F with storage and paid upgrades, a Loot only mode with no creature at all, and an admin panel to tune everything.

Version3.0.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin
HytaleUpdate 7
Java21
DependenciesNone. PerfectEconomy, Ecotale, EliteEssentials and PerfectHolograms 3.0 are optional

Buy on BuiltByBit

Installation

  1. Put PerfectSpawner-3.0.0.jar in your server's mods folder and start the server.
  2. Give yourself a spawner: /spawner give <you> Goblin_Miner, then place it and press F on it.
  3. Admins open /spawner admin to tune types, loot, upgrades and limits.

Updating from 2.0: replace the jar, nothing else. Spawners, stacks, owners, types and settings are kept, after a full copy of the data folder in backup-2.0.x-<date> on the first start. Spawners placed under 2.0 have no mode saved, so they start in Loot only: the owner switches them back to Living mobs on their page, or you change the mode new spawners start in, per type, in the admin panel (Types).

The spawner page

Press F on a spawner. Four tabs:

  • Overview: what the spawner does and why it waits (night only, full, storage full, chunk limit, no water...), the wave size, delay, creatures alive and the mode.
  • Storage: collect all, take one stack, sell all at the server's sell prices.
  • Upgrades: four tracks, five levels each, with the effect shown before you pay.
  • Access: owner, trusted players and their rights, hologram on or off, pick-up.

Modes

Living mobs: creatures appear within the spawn radius on a free spot (ground plus two blocks of air) and drop their loot when killed. Creatures made by a spawner are marked: counts stay exact after restarts and chunk unloads, and they are removed when their spawner is gone.

Loot only: no creature. Each wave rolls the creature's drops straight into the storage, 75 % of them by default (lootOnlyRatePercent, overridable per type). A full bag never drops anything on the ground: what does not fit stays in the spawner.

New spawners start in Loot only; the owner switches on the page. Flying and aquatic creatures start in Loot only. Aquatic creatures in Living mobs need water around the spawner (lava for the Lava Coelacanth); the page says how much is missing. At startup every creature is checked against the game: one that cannot be spawned in your game version is switched to Loot only, one that does not exist is disabled, and one console line lists them.

Upgrades

TrackLevels 2 to 5 (default)
SpeedWaves 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 % faster
Amount+1 / +2 / +3 / +4 creatures per wave
Storage512 / 1024 / 2048 / 4096 items (256 at level 1)
LootDrops x1.25 / x1.5 / x1.75 / x2

Prices to reach levels 2 to 5: 500, 1200, 2500, 5000 (upgradePrices). Money comes from PerfectEconomy, then Ecotale, then EliteEssentials, then an item (Gold Ingot by default). Everything is editable in the admin panel (Upgrades tab) or switched off with upgradesEnabled.

Pick-up and access

  • Stacking: place the same spawner on top of another, held in your main hand. A stack multiplies the wave size and the creature cap. maxStackSize is 8 by default (1 to 64).
  • Pick up from the page: one item that keeps its level, upgrades, stack and storage.
  • Breaking with a tool does the same for the owner and trusted players. breakRule decides for everyone else: owner (default), anyone, lost (destroyed, nothing comes back) or deny.
  • Trusted players per spawner, each with rights: collect and sell, upgrade, switch mode, pick up.
  • Limits: 10 spawners per player by default (perfectspawner.limit.N raises it), 6 active spawners per chunk, 40 creatures per chunk, 400 per world.

Spawner types

237 types, each with its own cage model: goblins, trorks, outlanders, skeletons, zombies, ferans, kweebecs, scarak, golems and spirits, animals, birds, fish and sea life, critters, and bosses. The admin panel groups them by family and has a search.

  • Boss spawners (Gobliterator, Feastmaster, Void Dragon, Shrouded Figure) are Admin only: /spawner give refuses them without perfectspawner.admin. They wait 300 seconds for a single creature by default.
  • Cinematic and placeholder creatures (Void Dragon, Shrouded Figure, Void Crystal, Tuluk Fisherman) work in Loot only and are tagged Experimental.
  • Goblin Scavenger, Goblin Hermit and Goblin Ogre no longer exist in the game. Spawners already placed keep working with the nearest goblin (Scrapper, Miner, Duke) and are tagged Legacy.

The cage shows its state from a distance: glowing embers when working, a red glow when full or blocked, dark when waiting.

Holograms

With PerfectHolograms 3.0 installed, the status hologram is a PerfectHolograms one: three lines (name, level and stack, state), turned to the front of the cage. Without it, a built-in nameplate is used. holograms can be auto, own or off. A hologram is shared by everyone, so it is written in the server language (English for a line the hologram font cannot draw).

Admin panel

Open with /spawner admin. Changes are a draft until you press Save.

  • Types: delay, creatures per wave, max alive, radius, day or night only, allowed modes, mode for new spawners, worlds, weighted creature list, tags.
  • Loot: source (game drop list, custom table or both), multiplier, Loot only yield, sell price of every item.
  • Upgrades: values, prices, currency.
  • World: every placed spawner, teleport, remove, clean orphan creatures.
  • Limits and Stats.

Per type (spawner_types.json)

Entities with weights, spawnDelaySeconds (20), spawnRadius (5), spawnCount (1), maxEntities (8, times the stack), timeRestriction (always, day or night), dropMultiplier, modes (both, mobs or loot), defaultMode (loot), lootSource (own, custom or both), lootOnlyRatePercent, worlds, dropList and the custom loot table. The admin panel edits all of them.

Skyblock mode

Spawners only work when a player is within activationRadius. On a Skyblock-style server with players on distant islands, set "requirePlayerNearby": false in config.json and keep a sensible max alive per type.

Languages

English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Simplified Chinese. Each player sees pages, messages and creature names in the language of their game. A config left on German or Italian falls back to English.

FAQ

My spawners from 2.0 stopped spawning creatures

They are in Loot only after the update (2.0 saved no mode). Press F on the spawner and switch to Living mobs, or set the mode for new spawners per type in /spawner admin (Types).

A spawner is not producing anything

Press F: the Overview tab says why it waits (night only, storage full, creature limit, no player nearby, no water...). The cage is dark when it waits and red when it is blocked.

The hologram text looks wrong

Check /spawner debug for the hologram provider. PerfectHolograms older than 3.0 is not used: the built-in nameplate takes over.

Can I use a custom creature?

Edit spawner_types.json by hand to point a type at another NPC role id, as long as the game can spawn it.

Can one spawner produce several creatures?

Yes. Add several entries to the type's creature list with their weights; one is picked at random each wave.
