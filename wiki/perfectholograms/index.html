PerfectHolograms

Version3.0.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin (Update 7)
LanguagesMenus and messages in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Chinese, each player in his game language
DependenciesNone (PerfectEconomy, PerfectJobs and PerfectScoreboard optional)

Floating text, items and leaderboards for your server, edited in game. Letters are 3D models, so you get hex colours, gradients, bold, italic, underline and 8 fonts, plus images, pages, animations, per-player lines and clickable holograms.

Buy on BuiltByBit

Installation

  1. Drop PerfectHolograms-3.0.0.jar into your server's mods/ folder, in place of the old jar
  2. Start or restart the server
  3. Type /ph in game
  4. Images: put the PNG (or animated GIF) in the plugin's images/ folder, add it to a hologram, and restart the server once so the image assets load

The editor

/ph (or /ph menu) opens the list of every hologram. It has a search box, filters (this world, near me, leaderboards, clickable, personal lines, created by plugins, hidden), sorting, a coloured preview, and the server cost: entities used against a budget (entityBudget). Per hologram: Edit, Teleport, Bring here, Copy here, Hide, Delete. + New starts from a template: blank, welcome sign, rules, richest players, top jobs, shop sign, event. Templates are written in the language of the admin who creates them.

The editor docks on the right and leaves the world visible. You edit a draft that only you see: players keep seeing the saved hologram until you press Save, and Cancel brings the saved version back. A new hologram appears in front of you at eye height, its text turned toward you. /ph edit <name> opens one hologram directly.

Line types

TypeWhat it is
TextRich text, see below
ItemA floating game item, spinning or still, in 3 sizes
LeaderboardPerfectEconomy richest players, or PerfectJobs total level or XP of a job. 3, 5 or 10 rows, podium colours, your own row format ({rank}, {name}, {value}), refreshed every 30 s, 1 min or 5 min
ImageA PNG or animated GIF from images/, or a link
BlankAn empty line for spacing

Pages: up to 6 per hologram, rotating every 5, 8, 10, 15 or 30 seconds (or not at all). Animations (colour wave, blink, typewriter, pulse) apply to one line, at slow, normal or fast speed, and run only while a player is near.

Rich text & fonts

SyntaxEffect
<#FF6B00>text</>Hex colour
<gradient:#FF0000,#0000FF>text</>Gradient between two or more colours
<rainbow>text</rainbow>Rainbow
<b> <i> <u>Bold, italic, underline

Eight fonts, chosen per hologram: Default, Sans, Serif, Thin, Cursive, Impact, Comic, Mono. The letters come from the plugin's glyph models: A-Z, digits, punctuation and the usual Western accents. Cyrillic and Chinese characters cannot be drawn inside a hologram.

Size & facing

Size goes from 2 to 100, 4 by default (defaultSize in config.json). The line spacing follows the size. Set it in the editor or with /ph size <name> <2-100>. Holograms made in 2.x keep their size: the number is the old scale value. Face me turns the text toward you, and /ph tp <name> puts you in front of a hologram, looking at it.

Personal lines

A line that contains {player}, {eco_balance...}, {eco_bank...}, {eco_rank} or a {job_...} placeholder shows each player his own value. The plugin spawns one copy of that line per player within 24 blocks. Limits in config.json: personalMaxPlayers (20) and personalMaxLines (8 per server). The editor shows the cost.

Clickable holograms

Players press F on the hologram. Actions: run a command as the player, run a command as the console (%player% is replaced by the name), show the next page, send a message, send a link. Each hologram has a cooldown, an optional permission and a hint text next to the F key. With an empty hint, players see the game's own "Press F to interact" in their language.

Visibility

  • View distance per hologram, 4 to 64 blocks, 32 by default
  • Show only to players with or without a permission
  • Hide a hologram without deleting it
  • Nothing is spawned for a hologram that nobody can see; images are only sent to players within 80 blocks

Languages

Every player reads the menus and messages in the language of his game: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Chinese. The lang key of config.json (and /ph setlang en-US, fr-FR...) is the language of the console and of the few texts shown in the world to everybody, such as the "level" and "XP" of a leaderboard row. The text you write inside a hologram stays as you wrote it. German and Italian are no longer offered because the game does not have them; an old config set to DE or IT uses English.

Configuration

config.json in the plugin's data folder. Missing keys are added with their default at startup.

KeyDefaultMeaning
langen-USConsole language and texts shown in the world
refreshSeconds5How often placeholders are refreshed
personalMaxPlayers20Players per personal line
personalMaxLines8Personal lines per server
entityBudget6000Entities shown against in the list's cost readout
defaultSize4Size of a new hologram

Holograms are stored in holograms.json. A wrong value no longer wipes the file; a broken file is kept aside and never overwritten, and saves are crash-safe.

For developers

Other plugins can create and control holograms through the plugin's manager, by reflection and without a dependency: create, setLines, setSize, getSize, setRotation, faceTowards, setSpacing, setOwner, delete. The 2.x methods keep their names and types. yaw follows the game's block rotation: 0 faces -Z, 90 faces -X, 180 faces +Z, 270 faces +X. Size is 2 to 100, and the line spacing follows it. A hologram created with setOwner carries a "Created by <plugin>" tag in the list; PerfectLootbox uses this for crate holograms (keep their plb_ name).

Updating from 2.x

Replace the jar with PerfectHolograms-3.0.0.jar and restart. Your holograms.json is converted at the first save, and the 2.0.3 file is kept as holograms.json.2.0.3-backup. Existing holograms keep their look and size. The 2.x commands and the 2.x permission still work.

FAQ

Image not showing?

Images need one server restart after you add them, so the image assets load. Only players within 80 blocks receive them. Images are not shown in the editor's draft.

Can I write Russian or Chinese in a hologram?

No. Letters in a hologram are models for Latin letters, digits, punctuation and Western accents. The menus themselves are translated.

How many holograms can I have?

Each letter is an entity. The list shows what your holograms use against entityBudget, and a hologram nobody can see spawns nothing.

The text of my hologram is turned the wrong way.

Open it in the editor and press Face me while you stand where players will read it.
