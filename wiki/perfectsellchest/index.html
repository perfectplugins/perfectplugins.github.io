PerfectSellChest

Version2.0.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin (Update 7)
LanguagesEnglish, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Simplified Chinese, each player in his game language
DependenciesNone (PerfectEconomy, Ecotale, EliteEssentials and PerfectShop optional)

Place a Sell Chest, fill it, and it sells what is inside every 30 seconds. The money goes to the player who placed the chest. The money is paid first and only then do the items leave the chest, so nothing is lost. It works with an economy plugin or without one.

Buy on BuiltByBit

Installation

  1. Stop the server and put PerfectSellChest-2.0.0.jar in your server's mods/ folder, in place of the old jar if you are updating
  2. Start the server. config.json and prices.json are created, with a starter price list
  3. Give yourself chests with /sellchest give <you> 2, or craft them at the Furniture bench
  4. Place a chest, put items in it and wait 30 seconds

Updating from 1.x

  • /sellchest now opens the menu. In 1.x it claimed the chest next to you. That is now /sellchest register, or the button "Claim the chest next to me" in the menu. Chests you place are still registered on their own, so the command is only for a chest that was there before the plugin was installed, or pasted with a prefab.
  • /sellchest setlang is gone: every player reads the plugin in the language of his game.
  • The conversion is automatic and happens once, on the first start. The 1.x files are copied to backup-1.2 first. Chests, earnings, limits, muted players and your prices are kept. The sell prices of the old PerfectEconomy shop are copied into the price list.
  • Owner protection is switched off on a server coming from 1.x, so nothing changes for your players, and on on a new server. Each part has its own switch in the settings.
  • Everybody can use /sellchest now. In 1.x only operators could.
  • A file with a typo in it no longer stops the server: it is set aside as .broken and the plugin starts with defaults.

The chests

  • Sell Chest: a merchant's chest in oak and brass, with a coin slot and a coin on the lid, a pair of scales on the front and a purse on the side. Recipe at the Furniture bench: 4 hardwood planks, 2 gold bars, 2 iron bars.
  • Sell Chest x2: ebony and gold with sapphire padding, two coins, two purses and a glowing sapphire, easy to tell from the simple chest in your hand, in your bag and in the world. Recipe: 1 Sell Chest, 6 gold bars, 1 ruby. It pays double (x2Multiplier).
  • Both chests are models made for the plugin, with their own animations: at rest the scales sway and a glint runs over the coin, the lid opens and shuts, and the chest goes back to rest once closed.
  • Admins can give chests with /sellchest give <player> [x2] [amount]. A setting turns crafting off, for servers that sell chests in a shop or a crate.
  • Only the owner can open and break his sell chests; staff with perfectsellchest.bypass or perfectsellchest.admin always can. Two switches in the settings.
  • A hologram above each chest, clear of the open lid, shows the owner and what it earned, in the language chosen by the admin.
  • Each player has a limit of chests (5 by default). Your own limit for a player is set in the admin panel; ranks use perfectsellchest.limit.<n> or perfectsellchest.limit.unlimited.

How selling works

  • Every 30 seconds (setting, 5 to 3600) each chest is worked out: the money is paid first, then the items leave the chest. If the economy refuses the payment, the items stay, the owner gets one message, and the chest tries again at the next round.
  • An item with no price stays in the chest. The owner gets one message naming it, and the chest shows "1 item with no price" in the menu.
  • Worn tools and armor are paid by durability. Broken ones stay in the chest.
  • Items with custom data (rolled weapons, bank notes) stay in the chest (keepItemsWithData).
  • The money goes to the owner even when he is offline. When he comes back, one line tells him what his chests earned while he was away.
  • Sale messages are grouped: one per round for all your chests. Each player picks chat, pop-up or off with /sellchest notify or in the menu.
  • With gold bars as money, a gold bar that does not fit in the owner's bag waits for him: Collect in /sellchest. Gold bars put in a chest are never sold.

Player menu and admin panel

/sellchest

  • My chests: each chest, where it is, what it earned, its last sale, its state, and the items nobody buys. "Forget this chest" for a chest in a deleted world, and "Claim the chest next to me".
  • Prices: what sells and for how much, in a Sell Chest and in a Sell Chest x2, with search and sorting.
  • History: your last 30 sales and the last 7 days.

/sellchest admin

  • Overview: chests, money paid today, in 7 days and in total, top sellers, and things to check (items with an unknown id, PerfectShop counting in another money).
  • Chests: every chest of the server, with search, teleport and "Remove from list".
  • Prices: edit the price list with an item picker, import the sell prices of PerfectShop, go back to the starter list.
  • Limits: the default limit, and players with their own limit, picked by name.
  • Settings: sell interval, x2 multiplier, money source, /shop cap, protection, crafting, holograms and their language, default message mode.

Money and other plugins

The money comes from the first of these that is installed: PerfectEconomy, Ecotale, EliteEssentials. If none is, chests pay in items: gold bars by default (economy.itemCurrency). Set economy.provider to perfecteconomy, ecotale, eliteessentials or items to force one. With PerfectEconomy 2 you choose the currency the chests pay in (perfectEconomyCurrency).

Every amount (menus, chat, pop-up, holograms, history) uses the symbol and the decimals of the economy in use, or the symbol of the item currency, and is written the way the reader's language writes numbers (1,250 in English, 1 250 in French, 1.250 in Spanish).

PerfectShop

With PerfectShop installed, a chest never pays more than 90 % of what the /shop asks for the same item, the x2 chest included (shopCap.factor, between 0.1 and 0.99). Buying in the shop and selling in a chest can no longer make money. The admin panel can import the sell prices of PerfectShop. If PerfectShop counts in another money than the chests, there is no cap and no import, and the admin overview says so.

Prices

A new server gets a starter list of 37 common items: ores, bars, gems, crops, wood, stone, hides and food. With gold bars as money the starter prices are divided by 10 (itemCurrencyPriceDivider), never below 0.01, and the fractions of a bar add up for each owner and are paid in whole bars.

The list is prices.json, a price per unit for each item id. It is easier to change it in game with /sellchest admin, Prices tab, which uses an item picker. Items not listed are not bought.

{
  "Ore_Iron": 4,
  "Ingredient_Bar_Gold": 50,
  "Plant_Crop_Pumpkin_Item": 6
}

Languages

Menus and messages follow the language of each player's game; there is no language command. The plugin ships English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Simplified Chinese. The hologram above a chest is seen by everybody, so it uses one language chosen by the admin (holograms.language in config.json, or the Settings tab). Item names come from the game.

FAQ

Items are not selling

Check the chest in /sellchest: its state says "Selling", or "1 item with no price" when an item is not on the price list. Put the price in /sellchest admin, Prices tab. A chest that was placed before the plugin was installed has to be claimed with /sellchest register while you stand next to it.

Do I need an economy plugin?

No. Without one, chests pay in gold bars. If PerfectEconomy, Ecotale or EliteEssentials is installed, it is used, in that order.

I used HTSkyBlock, EconomySystem or Ryukazan Economy

These are not supported. PerfectEconomy, Ecotale and EliteEssentials are; with none of them chests pay in gold bars.

Can a player make money by buying in the shop and selling in a chest?

Not with PerfectShop installed: a chest pays at most 90 % of the shop price. Without PerfectShop you set the prices yourself.

A player lost a chest after a world was deleted

The player can use "Forget this chest" in /sellchest, and an admin "Remove from list". Chests in a deleted world no longer count toward the limit forever.

A file was set aside as .broken

The file could not be read, so the plugin kept a copy named .broken next to it and started with defaults. Fix the typo in the copy and put it back, or start again from the admin panel.
