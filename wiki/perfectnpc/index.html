PerfectNPC

Version3.0.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin (Update 7)
LanguagesPages in English; chat messages in EN, FR, ES, DE, IT, PT (/pnpc lang)
DependenciesNone (PerfectEconomy, Ecotale or EliteEssentials optional)

NPCs that talk, trade and give quests. A dialog window with answers that do things, quest cards and a quest journal, a shop with daily offers, barter and selling that pays, and a full in-game editor: no file to edit, no code to type.

Buy on BuiltByBit

Installation

  1. Drop PerfectNPC-3.0.0.jar into your server's mods/ folder
  2. Start or restart the server
  3. Admins type /pnpc; players type /quests for their journal

Your first NPC

  1. Stand where the NPC should be and type /pnpc.
  2. Pick a template at the bottom: Blank, Trader, Quest Giver, Guard, Banker, Lore. The NPC appears where you stand.
  3. Click edit (the pencil). The editor has five tabs: Identity, Conversation, Shop, Quests, Behavior.
  4. Change what you want. Every change is a draft: Save applies it at once for your players, Cancel drops it.

Conversations

The dialog window sits at the bottom of the screen and is sized to its text. It shows the NPC's name and title, the text with a short typewriter effect (click the text to show it all) and numbered answers with an icon for what they do.

An NPC has pages; each page has text and a list of answers. Every answer has an action:

ActionWhat it does
Go to pageJumps to the page you choose
Next page / Previous pageMoves in the page order
Open shopOpens the NPC's shop
Offer questShows the quest card (Accept / Decline)
Turn in questChecks the objectives, takes the items, gives the rewards
Run a commandAs the player, or as the console ({player} is the player's name)
Open a linkOpens a web address
CloseCloses the window

Pages can have a title and a typewriter option. Texts accept the variables {username} (the player) and {npcname}, and the colour tags {b}, {i}, {#rrggbb}, {/}.

Conditions & rewards

Both are picked from lists in the editor. Under the hood they are readable texts in npcs.json.

Conditions (on an answer)

When it is not met, the answer is greyed with the reason ("Requires: Ember Guild member") or hidden, as you choose.

Other plugins can add their own conditions and rewards through the API (RequirementChecker, RewardProvider).

Quests

  • Objectives: kill creatures of a role, collect items, break blocks, place blocks.
  • Quest card: the player sees the objectives and the rewards with icons, then Accept or Decline.
  • Turn in at the NPC: the objectives are checked again, collected items are taken, then every reward is given (items, money, commands, tags, variables). "Collect" counts the items in the player's bag.
  • Markers over the NPC's head: [!] a quest to take, [?] a quest to turn in. Name plates are shared by all players, so the marker is the same for everyone.
  • Daily and weekly quests: weekly quests reset on weeklyResetDay (Monday by default) at the hour of dailyShopResetHour.
  • Journal: /quests - Active, Daily & weekly (with the time before reset) and Done. Progress bars, who to return to and where, rewards, and a button to abandon a quest.

Shops

  • Buy and Sell tabs, categories with the number of articles, your balance, and a "Back to <NPC>" button.
  • Today's offers: a rotating selection of articles. Off by default: set dailyShopEnabled to true in config.json; dailyShopItemCount is how many, dailyShopResetHour the reset hour.
  • Barter: an article can cost items instead of coins; the shop shows "you have" for each ingredient.
  • Quantity x1, x10 or Max, with the total and a confirmation.
  • Selling pays: each article has a sell price per item. Articles priced in coins with nothing to give back get half their buy price as sell price when a 2.x shop is converted.
  • Daily purchase limits per player, shown as "0 / 1 bought".
  • An NPC can open its shop directly when talked to (option "Open directly"), or through an answer with the action "Open shop".

Money

The shop (buy and sell), the money condition and the money reward work with no other plugin. The provider is chosen in the economy block of config.json (added at the first start of 3.0):

"economy": {
  "provider": "auto",
  "itemCurrency": { "itemId": "Ingredient_Bar_Gold", "displayName": "Gold", "symbol": "G" }
}
providerMeaning
autoPerfectEconomy, then Ecotale, then EliteEssentials, then items
perfecteconomy, ecotale, eliteessentialsForce that plugin
itemsMoney is an item (gold ingot by default): 1 item = 1 unit
noneNo money

The server log tells at startup which provider is used. With item money, prices are whole numbers (rounded up when the player pays, down when they receive; a sale worth less than one item is refused), your balance is the number of those items in your bag, and what you are owed when the bag is full waits in a per-player reserve (shop/itemReserve.json) until login or until there is room. It is never dropped and never lost.

Example: a currency exchanger

An NPC can swap any currency or item for another. Here, a money changer who turns 10 gold bars into 100 gems of PerfectEconomy (create a currency with the id gems in /eco first, or use your own id):

  1. /pnpc, create an NPC from the Blank template and open the editor, tab Conversation.
  2. Add an answer: "Exchange 10 gold bars for 100 gems". Action: Close (or Go to page, to a "Done" page).
  3. Condition: Item, Ingredient_Bar_Gold, amount 10. Show the answer greyed, so the player reads "Requires: 10 x Gold Bar".
  4. Reward 1: Take items, Ingredient_Bar_Gold, amount 10.
  5. Reward 2: Console command: eco give {player} 100 gems, label "100 gems".
  6. Save. Add one answer per rate (5 bars for 50 gems, and so on).

The same recipe works with any item and any command: pay items for a rank, a key, a kit, or a balance in another plugin. The condition guarantees the player carries the items; the first reward takes them before the command runs.

Not possible: the other way round (a balance for items) with a balance check, because there is no "take money" reward; use the shop with a coin price for that.

Manager & editor

/pnpc opens the manager: a list of your NPCs with search, type filter and world, and for each one edit, teleport, duplicate, delete (with a confirmation; a copy is kept in backups/deleted). Six templates create a ready NPC where you stand.

TabWhat you set
IdentityName, title, name plate, interaction hint, model, equipment (armor and weapons), position, preview
ConversationPages and answers, each with an action, a condition and a reward
ShopArticles (picked in a searchable item list), categories, prices, barter ingredients, sell prices, daily offers, daily limits
QuestsName, repeat, turn-in at the NPC, description, start condition, objectives (kill, collect, break, place), rewards, head marker
BehaviorFrozen, invulnerable, non hostile, attitude, idle animation, commands and messages when a player comes near

Tags and variables are typed in text fields so you can create new ones. Long lists use pages with arrows.

Updating from 2.x

Replace the jar and restart. At the first start every data file is copied to backups/before_3.0_<date>/, then converted once (nothing is converted again at the next start):

  • The five fixed dialog buttons (Next, Previous, Close, Custom 1, Custom 2) and the link button become normal answers, with their texts, player commands, console commands, requirements (items, consumed items, NPC prerequisites, custom checks), rewards and "last button" setting. Old answers keep their target page and command.
  • A merchant that opened its shop directly still does.
  • Coin-priced articles with nothing to give back get half their buy price as sell price.
  • The API (RequirementChecker, RewardProvider, QuestHandler) is unchanged.

FAQ

Does it need an economy plugin?

No. Without one, money is paid in gold ingots (editable in economy.itemCurrency). With PerfectEconomy (free), Ecotale or EliteEssentials it uses their balances.

Will my 2.x NPCs survive?

Yes. They are converted at the first start after a full copy in backups/before_3.0_<date>/. If something looks wrong, stop the server, restore the copy and send me the log.

My shop has no "Today's offers".

The rotation is off by default. Set dailyShopEnabled to true in config.json and mark the articles that may rotate as daily in the Shop tab.

Can an answer be shown only after a quest?

Yes: give it the condition "Quest state: completed", and choose greyed or hidden.

Why do the markers look the same for all players?

Hytale name plates are shared by every player, so [!] and [?] are the same for everyone.

NPC name plate doesn't support line breaks

This is a Hytale limitation: a name plate is a single line.

Player skin NPCs (humanoid characters)

Not supported: NPCs use the game's own models, picked in the Identity tab.
