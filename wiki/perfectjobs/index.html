PerfectJobs

Version2.0.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin (Update 7)
LanguagesPages in English; /jobs setlang EN, FR, ES, DE, IT, PT for the older texts
DependenciesNone (PerfectEconomy, Ecotale or EliteEssentials optional)

A complete job system: five jobs with 100 levels each, talent trees, milestone rewards and titles, boost potions and server events, daily quests, leaderboards and records, 20 hand-made tools, and a fishing game of its own (31 original fish, hook fight, journal, contests, treasures).

Installation

  1. Drop PerfectJobs-2.0.0.jar into your server's mods/ folder
  2. Start or restart the server
  3. Config files are generated on first run
  4. Players type /jobs; admins type /jobs admin

Features

  • 5 jobs: Miner, Lumberjack, Hunter, Farmer, Fisherman, 100 levels each
  • Talent trees: 3 columns x 4 tiers per job, one point every 2 levels, real effects
  • Milestones every 10 levels: money, items, commands and a job title
  • Boosts: server events and drinkable potions
  • Fishing: fish by zone, water, time and weather (lava too), tension fight, journal, contests, treasures
  • 20 hand-made tools in 4 ranks, 31 original fish, bobber and boost bottles
  • Daily quests (5 per job), specializations at level 25
  • Leaderboards and records per job and global
  • HUD in any of the 4 corners, admin panel in game
  • Economy: PerfectEconomy, Ecotale, EliteEssentials, or an item as money
  • Anti-exploit and crash-safe saves

Talent trees

One tree per job, 3 columns x 4 tiers (12 talents). Tiers open at levels 1 / 10 / 25 / 50. You earn one talent point every 2 levels (admins can add bonus points). Talents have ranks and prerequisites. Reset costs money (default 500; free when there is no economy).

All talents, costs, ranks and values are in talents.json and in the admin panel (Talents tab).

Milestones & titles

A milestone every 10 levels (interval editable, milestones.json): money, items, console commands and a job title. Rewards are given at level-up when they fit; otherwise they wait in /jobs > Rewards (never dropped, never lost). Players choose which title to show: placeholder {job_title}.

Boosts & events

  • Server event: /jobs event start <xp> [money] <minutes> [name]. XP and/or money multiplier for a duration, announced to everyone (title and chat) at start and end, survives a restart. /jobs event stop ends it.
  • Potions (right-click to drink): XP Tonic (XP x1.5, 30 min), Greater XP Tonic (XP x2, 60 min), Merchant's Draught (money x1.5, 30 min). Item ids Jobs_Boost_XP, Jobs_Boost_XP_Major, Jobs_Boost_Money; values in boosts.json. Give them with /jobs giveboost <player> <item> [amount].
  • Active boosts are shown in /jobs and in the HUD with the time left.

Fishing

What bites, where and when

31 original fish in six rarities (commons, freshwater rares, tropicals, jellyfish, crustaceans, legendaries) and sizes XS to XXL, each with a weight in kg. The catch depends on the zone of the world (Emerald Wilds, Howling Sands, Whisperfrost Frontiers, Devastated Lands, open ocean, and regions such as The Fens), sea or fresh water, depth, night or day and rain or snow. You can also fish in lava (Embertrout, Magma Stonefin, and in the Devastated Lands the Cinderfang Pike and the Sunswallower). Commons bite everywhere, so a spot is never empty. Worlds without the Hytale world generator (flat, void) have no zone: only species without a zone condition bite there. Every condition is editable per species in fishing.json.

The hook fight

At the bite, right-click to hook, then right-click at the right rhythm to reel in (gauge under the crosshair). Too fast: the tension climbs and the line snaps. Too slow: the line goes slack and the fish escapes. When the fish runs, let it go. Rare, legendary and big fish fight harder; commons can never snap. Set "hookMode": "simple" in fishing.json for the old one-click catch.

Angler's journal

/jobs journal or the Journal tab: the 31 species, a silhouette until caught, then name, habitat, size record, number caught and where you caught the first. Completing a category gives a reward (editable).

Fishing contests

/jobs contest start <minutes> <biggest|most> [name], /jobs contest stop (rewards for the first 3) or cancel, or from /jobs admin > Boosts. Live ranking on screen, announcements at start and end. A contest survives a restart; one that ended while the server was off is closed at the next start. Prizes are kept until the winner is online with room in the inventory. /jobs contest shows the contest in progress.

Treasures and junk

A small chance to fish up junk, a message in a bottle (lore notes of the game), lost equipment or a sunken treasure (a loot roll of the game's chests for that zone, better with a better rod), depending on sea, fresh water or lava. Chances are editable in fishing.json.

Tools

Each job has a hand-made tool in 4 ranks: T1 wood and stone, T2 iron, T3 ornate steel, T4 precious metal with a gem. Players claim or upgrade their tool in the Jobs page when they reach level 1 / 25 / 50 / 75. Item ids: Jobs_Pickaxe_T1..T4, Jobs_Axe_T1..T4, Jobs_Sword_T1..T4, Jobs_Hoe_T1..T4, Jobs_FishingRod_T1..T4.

Daily quests

Each job generates 5 daily quests, scaled to your level, rewarding XP and money. Quest money is paid for every job. Admins can write their own templates (quest_templates.json or the admin panel). The fisherman's quests only ask for species that bite in any water.

Economy

Provider setting economy.provider in config.json: auto (default), perfecteconomy, ecotale, eliteessentials, items or none. Auto picks PerfectEconomy > Ecotale > EliteEssentials > items. With no economy plugin, money is paid as an item (default Gold Ingot, Ingredient_Bar_Gold, 1 item = 1 money): fractions add up per player, a full item is given as soon as it reaches 1, and items that do not fit wait in /jobs > Rewards. none = XP only.

Admin panel

Open with /jobs admin (steel-blue pages): XP per action, talent trees, milestones, boosts and server event (and fishing contests), players (levels, joined jobs, talent points, resets), settings, and a button to reload the files.

Configuration

Files are stored in the plugin's data folder (UserData/Saves/MODDING/mods/KatsuyaTV_PerfectJobs/): config.json, jobs.json, quest_templates.json, talents.json, milestones.json, boosts.json, boost_event.json, fishing.json, fishing_contest.json, players.json, records/, backups/.

config.json (main settings)

{
  "lang": "EN",
  "xpBoost": 1.0,
  "maxJobsPerPlayer": 1,
  "hudEnabled": true,
  "hudPosition": "TopRight",
  "creativeGivesXp": false,
  "ignorePlacedBlocks": true,
  "levelUpAnnounce": false,
  "antiExploitEnabled": true,
  "economy": { "provider": "auto",
               "itemCurrency": { "itemId": "Ingredient_Bar_Gold", "displayName": "Gold", "symbol": "G" } }
}

hudPosition: TopLeft, TopRight, BottomLeft or BottomRight. ignorePlacedBlocks: no XP for a block you just placed (stops place / break loops).

Updating from 1.x

Replace the jar and restart. At the first start your 1.x data is converted after a full copy in backups/before_2.0_<date>/: levels, XP, specializations, stats, tools, daily quests and records are kept. Milestones already passed are marked as reached (titles unlocked, past rewards not handed out) and talent points are given for your current level. The records files get the fishing journal keys at the first start, after a copy in backups/before_journal_<date>/; a category already complete before the update can be claimed in the journal.

FAQ

Does it need an economy plugin?

No. Without one, money is paid as an item (gold ingots by default). With PerfectEconomy (free), Ecotale or EliteEssentials it pays real money.

Why can my players only join one job?

The default limit is 1. Raise maxJobsPerPlayer or give perfectjobs.jobs.max.2, .3 ... or .unlimited to a rank.

Nothing bites in my fishing world.

Worlds without the Hytale world generator (flat, void) have no zones: only species with no zone condition bite. Edit fishing.json to change conditions.

Can I go back to the one-click fishing?

Yes: "hookMode": "simple" in fishing.json.

Can I change talents, rewards and XP?

Yes, everything in the admin panel or in the JSON files; use /jobs reload after editing files by hand.

Can players farm XP by placing and breaking blocks?

No: a block placed by a player gives no XP when broken (setting ignorePlacedBlocks). No XP in Creative either (creativeGivesXp), and none when a protection plugin refuses the break.
