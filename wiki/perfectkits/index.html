PerfectKits

Starter kits, daily rewards and rank kits with a real player menu and a full admin panel. Cooldowns, uses per player and in total, permissions, first-join kits, announcements and console commands, all built in game from your own inventory.

Version1.2.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin (Update 7)
LanguagesEN, FR (everything); ES, DE, IT (commands and old texts, new pages in English)
DependenciesNone

Buy on BuiltByBit

Installation

  1. Drop PerfectKits-1.2.0.jar in mods/
  2. Start the server
  3. Admins use /kit admin to build a kit; players use /kit

Features

  • Player menu -- every kit with its state: ready, countdown, or locked with the reason
  • Exact item copies -- items taken from your inventory keep stats, enchantments, durability and custom data (PerfectItemManager weapons keep their stats)
  • Cooldown -- seconds, minutes, hours or days
  • Uses -- per player (0 = unlimited, 1 = once ever) and in total on the server
  • Permission -- optional, one per kit
  • First-join kit -- given when the player joins
  • Announce -- tell everybody when a kit is claimed
  • Commands on claim -- up to 6 console commands, with %player% / {player} and %kit% / {kit}
  • Bag full -- items wait in the menu (Collect), never dropped or lost
  • Safe -- crash-safe saves, no double claim

Player menu

/kit opens the menu, /kit <kit> claims a kit directly. Open to every player by default. The menu shows, for each kit: its state (ready, ready in 3 h 10 min, locked with the reason: needs a rank, used up, all claimed on the server), its content (click an item to see its name, special items are marked), your uses left, the server stock, extra rewards and a Claim button. Countdowns update by themselves. Items that do not fit in the bag wait in the menu (Collect button) and the player is reminded when he joins.

Admin panel

/kit admin (permission perfectkits.admin). You edit a draft and press Save or Cancel; leaving with unsaved changes asks first.

  • Kits tab -- list of kits and the editor: display name, command name, description, icon, cooldown, sort order, uses per player, total uses, first-join kit, announce, claims count (with reset), one-line preview. Duplicate and Delete (asks first).
  • Items -- up to 42 per kit, from your inventory (exact copies) or from the list of every game item; quantity, order, remove.
  • Commands -- up to 6 console commands run at each claim.
  • Players tab -- search, every kit of a player (uses, last claim, next claim), reset one kit or all, give a kit to an online player.
  • Settings tab -- language, max kits waiting at once, show or hide locked kits.

Data & update

Files in mods/KatsuyaTV_PerfectKits/: kits.json (kits), players.json (uses, last claims, waiting items), config.json. Writes go through a temporary file. Updating from 1.1: replace the jar; kits and cooldowns are converted at the first start after a copy in backups/before_1.2_<date>/. One-time kits become "1 use per player"; each 1.1 cooldown counts as one claim at that time; players known by 1.1 do not get first-join kits again. cooldowns.json is kept but no longer read.

FAQ

/kit only shows an error on Update 7?

Update 7 ships developer macro commands (/kit iron, /kit workbench1...) that take over /kit. PerfectKits 1.2.0 takes it back at startup and after every reload of those macros (log line: "/kit taken back from MacroCommandCollection"). The macros are hidden while the plugin runs.

A kit is named like a sub-command (list, give...)?

Claim it with /kit claim <kit>.

The bag is full?

Nothing is lost: the items wait in the menu, press Collect when there is room.

How do I make a one-time starter kit?

In the editor set uses per player to 1 and tick first-join kit.
