PerfectOneBlock

Version2.0.0
PlatformHytale Server Plugin (Update 7)
LanguagesEnglish, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Chinese, each player in his game language
DependenciesNone (PerfectEconomy, Ecotale, EliteEssentials, PerfectHolograms 3.x and PerfectShop optional)

A complete OneBlock gamemode: break the one block, a new one takes its place. 10 phases that follow the regions of the game, a guardian at the end of each phase, real chests in four rarities, a visible lucky block, prestige, quests, a shop, an auction house and teams. Everything is built in: drop the JAR and play.

Installation

  1. Stop the server and put PerfectOneBlock-2.0.0.jar in your server's mods/ folder, in place of the old jar if you are updating
  2. Start the server. The void world for the islands is created for you
  3. Players type /ob, create their island and break the block

Updating from 1.x

  • Islands, balances, quests and settings of 1.1 and 1.2 are kept. On the first start the whole data folder is copied to backup-before-2.0-<date> before anything is converted.
  • Islands of the 5 old phases move to the matching phase of the 10, never backward: an island halfway through Borea starts halfway through Frostmarch Tundra. The guardians of the phases before are counted as beaten, and an island that could prestige still can.
  • A phases.json, shop.json or lucky_rewards.json you changed is kept. The 2.0 files are written next to it (phases.default.json, shop.default.json). Island sizes, upgrade costs, prestige multipliers and the daily reward you had changed are carried into the new files.
  • The 1.x shop sold and bought blocks that breaking never gives (breaking Ore_Copper_Stone gives Ore_Copper). The 2.0 shop buys what you really mine.
  • Prestige no longer clears the island, only the run of phases starts again.
  • A file the plugin cannot read is never overwritten: a copy is kept next to it and the console says so.

Phases

One phase per region of the game, in the order of the zones. The name is the game's own, in each player's language. Each phase has its blocks (two of them rare), its creatures, its guardian and its chest chances. After the last phase the island starts again at phase 1 in cycle 2, with stronger guardians and a few more chests.

Every creature of a phase is a real Hytale creature and appears next to the block. Phases, blocks, creatures and chances are in phases.json and in the admin editor. The next block is shown in the HUD and above the block (two blocks from prestige 2). /ob skip replaces a block you cannot break, once every 2 minutes. Sounds, particles and a region title mark new blocks, rare blocks, chests, new phases and prestige; each player can turn them off for himself.

Guardians

A red crystal seal takes the place of the block at the end of a phase. Break it to call the guardian (it has more health with more teammates around), or postpone it for 100 blocks with /ob guardian postpone. Beat it for an Epic chest (Legendary for the last phase) and coins shared by the team. The guardians of phases 8 and 9 also give the pickaxe the next ores need.

Chests and lucky block

Real chests in four rarities (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary) appear on the block, with the game's loot for the region and coins. Open them, or hit one once to take everything. Legendary chests roll the region's loot twice and add one bonus line (onyxium, prisma, mithril or a gem): onyxium and prisma come from them only. The lucky block is a glowing yellow crystal: items, coins, farm animals, a trap or a chest. Items that do not fit in your bag go to your loot box (54 stacks), /ob collect.

Rarities and loot are in chests.json, lucky block rewards in lucky_block.json, guardians in guardians.json. New islands get a starter kit (crude pickaxe, hatchet, shovel and bread); the 15th block is a chest and the 60th a lucky block.

Island, value, upgrades

  • Island value: every block you build counts (gold bricks and gems a lot, dirt very little). Two rankings: island value and blocks mined. /ob value recount counts again.
  • Five upgrades: island size, team size, chest luck, creature loot and a visible island border. Some levels need a prestige. Costs and levels are in upgrades.json.
  • Protection: chests, doors and benches are protected from visitors; the one block can only be broken by the island's team. PvP follows the island where the hit player stands. Falling into the void brings you back to your island.
  • Reset: /ob reset starts your island again on the same spot; your team, bank and quests stay.

Prestige and daily streak

Prestige has 10 levels. It keeps your island and your upgrades: only the run of phases starts again. Each level raises sell prices and loot and unlocks something (bigger islands, a second preview block, better guardian chests). Prestige X is announced to the whole server. /ob prestige asks first, /ob prestige confirm does it. Levels are in prestige.json.

The daily streak goes up to x2.5 on the 7th day, with a chest on day 7; a missed day only takes one day off the streak. The daily reward belongs to the player, so resetting or changing island does not give it again. See daily.json.

Team and visitors

Rights for Member, Trusted and Visitor (team_permissions.json, or the Team page), team chat with /ob tc, and /ob kick, /ob ban, /ob unban, /ob bans for visitors. You can invite any online player; the invitation lasts 5 minutes. Members can build, break and open chests on their island.

Economy and other plugins

No other plugin is needed. PerfectEconomy is used first, then Ecotale, then EliteEssentials, then OneBlock's own money. A server coming from 1.1 that has OneBlock balances keeps OneBlock's money; set "economyProvider": "auto" in config.json to switch. Money is counted in cents.

  • PerfectHolograms 3.x draws the text above the block, updated in place at most once a second. Without it OneBlock uses its own text.
  • PerfectShop: its auction house (/ah) is the one players use. The Auction house tab goes away and /ob auction opens /ah. Listings already made in OneBlock can still be listed, cancelled and collected (/ob auction my, cancel, claim). The OneBlock shop stays.
  • Without PerfectShop, /ob auction sell <price> lists the stack in your hand. It leaves your bag, keeps its wear and quality, and comes back if the listing is cancelled or expires. What does not fit in your bag waits for /ob auction claim.
  • The shop takes the items before paying when you sell, and checks there is room in your bag when you buy. Buy by category in x1, x10 or x64; sell one, a stack or everything.

Menus, HUD and admin editor

/ob opens one window with a menu on the left: Island, Phases, Quests, Upgrades, Shop, Auction house, Team, Islands and Settings. /ob shop, /ob quests, /ob auction, /ob settings and /ob team open it on that tab.

The HUD is top left by default. Admins choose its position (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right, center-right, or hidden) with /ob admin hud <position>; each player can hide it with /ob hud.

/ob admin opens the admin editor. Phases, chests, lucky block, guardians, shop, upgrades, prestige, daily streak and island value can be changed in game, checked, saved and applied without a restart. An Islands tab finds an island and can teleport you to it, recount its value, change its phase, reset its guardian, give or take money, or delete it.

Languages

Every chat message, the HUD and the menus follow the language of each player's game: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Ukrainian and Simplified Chinese. Item and creature names come from the game. The text above the block uses the server language (lang in config.json, or /ob setlang en-US, fr-FR, es-ES, pt-BR, ru-RU, uk-UA, zh-CN; EN, FR and ES still work).

Files are in the plugin's data folder. Everything can also be changed in game with /ob admin.

chests.json, guardians.json, lucky_block.jsonRarities and loot, the 10 guardians, lucky block rewards

Quests have 7 types (mine, sell, earn, kill, reach a phase, upgrade, prestige) and all of them count. Daily quests change at the hour set in quests.json.

FAQ

Can I keep my own phases?

Yes. A phases.json you changed is kept when you update from 1.x. The 2.0 phases are written next to it as phases.default.json.

Do I need PerfectEconomy?

No. OneBlock has its own money. If PerfectEconomy, Ecotale or EliteEssentials is installed, it is used instead.

Where did my auction listings go after installing PerfectShop?

They are still there. Use /ob auction my, cancel and claim; new listings go through /ah.

A file was not loaded, what happened?

A file the plugin cannot read is never overwritten. A copy is kept next to it and the console tells you which one.
